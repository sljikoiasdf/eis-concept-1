Dine at home

Dinner at your own table, cooked and served by a master of Japanese food.

From $100 a head.

Enquire

Five to ten guests, and up to twelve seated. Larger groups by arrangement.

Tuesday to Saturday, lunch or dinner.

Inner Melbourne, within 15 km of Albert Park. Travel is included.

On the night

Hiro arrives about two hours before the first course with the ingredients, his knives and the serving plates.

He cooks in your kitchen, serves each course, and tells your guests what they are eating.

He stays about three hours from the first course, then leaves the kitchen as he found it.

What you provide

  • a stove that works
  • a bench about 1.5 metres long
  • a shelf in the fridge
  • your table, chairs, cutlery and glasses

Dietaries and drinks

We ask about dietary needs when you book and we handle them. Tell us seven days ahead if anything changes. Gluten-free is well covered.

Wine, sake and non-alcoholic drinks can be matched to the menu from $45 a head. Or bring your own bottles; there is no charge for opening them.
