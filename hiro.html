Hiroyasu Aoki was born in Tokyo in 1974 and has cooked professionally since the early 1990s. He spent twenty-five years at 188 Bridport Street in Albert Park. He now cooks in homes and workplaces across Melbourne.

“Elegant and refined food is possible in Japanese cuisine, and this relies on a chef’s training and creativity, technique, ingredient, freshness of produce and presentation on the plate.”

Tokyo 1974.
Albert Park 2001.

  1. 1974

    Born in Tokyo. High school in Kanagawa Prefecture, south of the city.

  2. Early 1990s

    Trained at the Tsuji Culinary Institute in Abeno, Osaka.

  3. 1994

    Düsseldorf, at the Benkei Restaurant in the Nikko Hotel, under Chef Matsushiro.

  4. 1995

    Taipei.

  5. 1996

    Melbourne. Suntory first, then Aya.

  6. August 2001

    Opened Eis at 188 Bridport Street, Albert Park.

  7. 2008

    Architects EAT redesigned the interior and the brand.

  8. 2001 to 2026

    Twenty-five years of continuous trade at the one address, with a wine list built across those years.

  9. 2026

    Closed the restaurant and took Eis to the client.

188 Bridport Street

Eis opened in August 2001 and traded at 188 Bridport Street for twenty-five years.

Architects EAT redesigned the interior and the brand in 2008.

The room held private dinners, celebrations, corporate dinners and lunch meetings for up to 36 people.

“After 25 years of being loved and supported by our loyal guests, Eis enters a new chapter. We remain close to the guests who have supported us over the years, continuing to share Chef Aoki’s passion for Japanese cuisine across a variety of occasions and settings.”

Hiroyasu Aoki, 2026
